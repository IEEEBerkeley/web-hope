It is important that you attend even if you already have a group, as we have made some changes to how project groups are set up. In particular:

We want all project groups to have 4 enrolled students. If your group can't find 4 people, speak to us during lecture.

We will ask all groups (once they have 4 enrolled students) to submit the project group form.

If your group already has 4 enrolled students, you will just need to resubmit the project form and specify the 4 enrolled students.

If an auditor (somebody not officially enrolled in the course) wants to join your group, you can put them down separately (NOT as an enrolled student)

We will give more information during lecture and in the coming days.
