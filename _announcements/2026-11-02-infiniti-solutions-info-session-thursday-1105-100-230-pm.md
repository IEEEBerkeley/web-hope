As you may know, IEEE (the campus parent organization that runs HOPE) also holds other interesting events throughout the semester that are relevant for engineering students.

Working on your HOPE or APE DeCal final project? Designing your own electronics, or simply curious about PCB manufacturing? Ever wonder how your PCB design can become a real, mass assembled product?

Join us for an info session with Infiniti Solutions to explore how electronics go from design to production!

About Infiniti Solutions: Infiniti Solutions USA, Inc. delivers world-class Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) to Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), specializing in PCB layout for burn-in and logic boards, contract manufacturing, and semiconductor reliability testing. From low-volume prototyping to high-volume production, their global capabilities support the full electronics manufacturing lifecycle.

What to Expect: Discover how PCBAs are manufactured and assembled at scale, and explore the role of semiconductor reliability and burn-in systems in real-world electronics manufacturing!
